STEP 1: FLATTENING LOOKUP TABLES (Attribute Translation)

Let's Replace foreign keys with actual descriptive attributes.

Original (Normalized):
PRODUCTS

PRODUCT_ID

PRODUCT_DESC

CATEGORY_ID (FK)

UPDATE_DT

PRODUCT_CATEGORIES (Separate lookup table)

CATEGORY_ID (PK)

CATEGORY_NAME

UPDATE_DT

Denormalized (Star Schema):
DIM_PRODUCT

PRODUCT_ID (PK)

PRODUCT_DESC

CATEGORY_ID (Kept for drill-down)

CATEGORY_NAME (DENORMALIZED - brought from lookup table)

UPDATE_DT (REMOVED - ETL metadata)

We Eliminate the join between PRODUCTS and PRODUCT_CATEGORIES. Can filter by CATEGORY_NAME directly without a join.

STEP 2: SNOWFLAKE COLLAPSING (Hierarchy Flattening)

Now let's collapse multi-level snowflake hierarchies into a single dimension.

Original (Normalized - 5 tables):
ADDRESSES → CITIES → COUNTRIES → REGIONS
↘ ECONOMIC_REGIONS

Denormalized (Star Schema):
DIM_CUSTOMER

CUSTOMER_ID (PK)

FIRST_NAME

LAST_NAME

EMAIL

ADDRESS (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

POSTAL_CODE (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

CITY_ID (Kept for grain)

COUNTRY_ID (from COUNTRIES)

COUNTRY_CODE (DENORMALIZED - from COUNTRIES)

REGION_ID (from REGIONS)

REGION (DENORMALIZED - from REGIONS)

ECONOMIC_REGION_ID (from ECONOMIC_REGIONS)

ECONOMIC_REGION (DENORMALIZED - from ECONOMIC_REGIONS)

PHONE (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

DIM_STORE

STORE_ID (PK)

MANAGER_ID

ADDRESS (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

CITY_ID

POSTAL_CODE (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

COUNTRY_CODE (DENORMALIZED - from COUNTRIES)

REGION (DENORMALIZED - from REGIONS)

ECONOMIC_REGION (DENORMALIZED - from ECONOMIC_REGIONS)

PHONE (DENORMALIZED - from ADDRESSES)

We replaced 5-6 table joins with a single table scan. Geographic analysis becomes a simple WHERE clause.

STEP 4: DEGENERATE DIMENSION HANDLING

Goal: Preserve atomic transaction identifiers without creating separate dimension tables.

Original (Normalized):
SALES (Header/Transaction table)
PAYMENTS (Child/Line Item table)

In a pure normalized model, these would require:
DIM_SALE and DIM_PAYMENT dimension tables

Denormalized (Star Schema):
FACT_SALES

SALE_ID (DEGENERATE DIMENSION - kept in fact table)

PAYMENT_ID (DEGENERATE DIMENSION - kept in fact table)

AMOUNT

(all other foreign keys)

Result: No attributes exist for SALE_ID or PAYMENT_ID beyond the ID itself. Creating DIM_SALE or DIM_PAYMENT with just an ID wastes space and join performance. Keeping them as degenerate dimensions in the fact table allows:

Counting distinct sales (COUNT DISTINCT SALE_ID)

Grouping by transaction

Reconciling to source systems

Preserving grain (1 row = 1 payment line item)
