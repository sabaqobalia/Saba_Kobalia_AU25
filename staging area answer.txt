The staging area is a temporary layer where raw data from sources like source1.csv is collected and prepared before entering the data warehouse. Its purpose is to safely store the data, clean and standardize formats, handle missing values, and add technical information like load timestamps. Data cannot be loaded directly into the 3NF layer because source data is often inconsistent, unclean, or not fully normalized, which would violate the integrity and structure required by the 3NF model.
