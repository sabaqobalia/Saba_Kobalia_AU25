Unlike the star schema where we fully denormalize, for a snowflake schema we intentionally STOP at partial denormalization. Here are the 4 steps applied to create the snowflake schema:

STEP 1: PARTIAL FLATTENING OF LOOKUP TABLES (Attribute Translation - STOPPED)

Goal is to remove transactional metadata but PRESERVE lookup table foreign keys. Do NOT bring descriptive attributes into the main dimension table.

Original (Normalized):
PRODUCTS

PRODUCT_ID

PRODUCT_DESC

CATEGORY_ID (FK)

UPDATE_DT

PRODUCT_CATEGORIES

CATEGORY_ID (PK)

CATEGORY_NAME

UPDATE_DT

Snowflake Schema:
DIM_PRODUCT

PRODUCT_ID (PK)

PRODUCT_DESC

CATEGORY_ID (FK) -- PRESERVED - not denormalized

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED (ETL metadata)

DIM_PRODUCT_CATEGORY

CATEGORY_ID (PK)

CATEGORY_NAME

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED (ETL metadata)

Why we STOP here:
Star schema would bring CATEGORY_NAME into DIM_PRODUCT. Snowflake schema preserves the foreign key and keeps the lookup table separate. Maintains normalization to save storage when many products share few categories. Accepts some joins for hierarchical queries.

STEP 2: SNOWFLAKE PRESERVATION (Hierarchy Maintenance)

We keep the normalized geographic hierarchy intact. Do not collapse into a single dimension.

Original (Normalized):
ADDRESSES → CITIES → COUNTRIES → REGIONS
↘ ECONOMIC_REGIONS

Snowflake Schema (Preserved):
DIM_ADDRESS

ADDRESS_ID (PK)

ADDRESS

CITY_ID (FK) -- PRESERVED - references DIM_CITY

POSTAL_CODE

PHONE

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

DIM_CITY

CITY_ID (PK)

CITY_NAME

COUNTRY_ID (FK) -- PRESERVED - references DIM_COUNTRY

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

DIM_COUNTRY

COUNTRY_ID (PK)

COUNTRY_CODE

REGION_ID (FK) -- PRESERVED - references DIM_REGION

ECONOMIC_REGION_ID (FK) -- PRESERVED - references DIM_ECONOMIC_REGION

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

DIM_REGION

REGION_ID (PK)

REGION

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

DIM_ECONOMIC_REGION

ECONOMIC_REGION_ID (PK)

ECONOMIC_REGION

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

Star schema collapses all 5 tables into DIM_CUSTOMER and DIM_STORE with denormalized attributes. Snowflake schema PRESERVES the entire 5-level hierarchy. Each level remains a separate physical table.We have Less data redundancy, single point of maintenance, strict hierarchy enforcement. But, queries now require 5 joins to navigate from address to economic region.

Applied to all dimensions with addresses:
DIM_CUSTOMER: ADDRESS_ID (FK) → DIM_ADDRESS
DIM_STORE: ADDRESS_ID (FK) → DIM_ADDRESS
DIM_EMPLOYEE: ADDRESS_ID (FK) → DIM_ADDRESS

No denormalization of address attributes into these dimensions.

STEP 3: REMOVING TRANSACTIONAL METADATA (ETL Attribution)

Goal: Strip operational tracking fields not relevant to business analysis. This step is IDENTICAL to star schema.

Original (Normalized):
PAYMENTS

PAYMENT_ID

CUSTOMER_ID

EMPLOYEE_ID

SALE_ID

AMOUNT

UPDATE_DT -- Operational timestamp

SALES

SALE_ID

SALES_DATE

PRODUCT_ID

CUSTOMER_ID

STORE_ID

EMPLOYEE_ID

UPDATE_DT -- Operational timestamp

Also in every other table:
PRODUCTS.UPDATE_DT
PRODUCT_CATEGORIES.UPDATE_DT
EMPLOYEES.UPDATE_DT
STORES.UPDATE_DT
CUSTOMERS.UPDATE_DT
ADDRESSES.UPDATE_DT
CITIES.UPDATE_DT
COUNTRIES.UPDATE_DT
REGIONS.UPDATE_DT
ECONOMIC_REGIONS.UPDATE_DT

Snowflake Schema:
FACT_SALES

SALE_ID -- Degenerate Dimension

SALES_DATE_ID -- FK to DIM_DATE

PRODUCT_ID -- FK

CUSTOMER_ID -- FK

STORE_ID -- FK

EMPLOYEE_ID -- FK

PAYMENT_ID -- Degenerate Dimension

AMOUNT -- MEASURE

UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED

ALL dimension tables:
UPDATE_DT -- REMOVED from every dimension table

Why this is the same:
UPDATE_DT is operational, not analytical, regardless of schema style. Both star and snowflake remove these timestamps. 

STEP 4: DEGENERATE DIMENSION HANDLING

Goal: Preserve atomic transaction identifiers without creating separate dimension tables. This step is IDENTICAL to star schema.

Original (Normalized):
SALES (Transaction header)

SALE_ID (PK)

PAYMENTS (Transaction line items)

PAYMENT_ID (PK)

SALE_ID (FK)

Snowflake Schema:
FACT_SALES

SALE_ID -- DEGENERATE DIMENSION (kept in fact table)

PAYMENT_ID -- DEGENERATE DIMENSION (kept in fact table)

AMOUNT

(all other foreign keys)

Why this is the same:
No descriptive attributes exist for SALE_ID or PAYMENT_ID. Creating DIM_SALE or DIM_PAYMENT would be empty shells with just IDs. This wastes storage and adds unnecessary joins. Degenerate dimensions in the fact table are the optimal solution for both star and snowflake. Preserves grain (1 row = 1 payment line item).
